    Remove SynthID

    Presets mirror the published reverse-SynthID attack research. Most image stages run a quality check, except local shifts and rotation which always apply. Video stages use FFmpeg approximations at a fixed MP4 quality without PSNR gating or edge-preserving smoothing, so the re-encode rounds, re-encode quality, quality floor (PSNR), and smoothing settings apply to images only. Luma noise settings that round to zero whole levels are skipped for video (the filter adds integer noise only), and other fractional values are rounded.
